Printing tips
  • Filament: plain white or natural PLA. Very bright white (heavily pigmented) can block light; coloured and silk filaments wash out the picture.
  • Slicer: 100 % infill (no pattern showing through), 0.12–0.16 mm layers, slow outer walls.
  • Orientation: stand it upright on its bottom edge with a brim. Curved ones are exported standing up already.
  • Light: a white LED (about 300–600 lumens) a few centimetres behind it.
  • Test first: set the width to about 2" and print a small piece to check your filament.
  • Too dark? Lower max thickness or raise Brightness. Washed-out highlights? Raise min thickness.
  • Lamp shade: print standing on its bottom edge, no supports, 100 % infill. Set the lamp diameter so it slips over your LED puck light (or use the bottom lip to rest on it). With a lamp fitter, use an LED bulb only: PLA softens at about 60 °C.
  • Shapes with a hanging hole: print flat on the bed (picture side up) or upright with a brim.
